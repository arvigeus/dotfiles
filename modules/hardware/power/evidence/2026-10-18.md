# Physical GA402RK investigation — 2026-10-18

Status: implemented in the repository and installed on the GA402RK. The selected
Performance parameters passed separate 15-minute GPU and combined-load trials;
actual gaming stability and the historical shutdown cause remain unproven.
Post-reboot configuration, both power-source transitions, the final repeated
battery comparison and a manual suspend/resume have passed verification.
The October 1 investigation used another installation on the same machine.
Its conclusions are hypotheses for this installation, not a baseline.

## Observer-effect correction

The first Chromium trial is invalid for power conclusions. The diagnostic
recorder sampled active dGPU hwmon sensors every two seconds, faster than its
five-second autosuspend delay. Checking `runtime_status` before reading sensors
prevents waking an already-suspended GPU, but does not stop repeated active
reads from extending its awake period.

In a controlled check with the **same browser still running**, the diagnostic
sampler was paused and a passive runtime-state recorder ran for 20.8 seconds.
The dGPU was active for the first four samples and suspended for the remaining
sixteen. No browser setting was changed. This disproves the initial suspicion
that Chromium necessarily keeps the dGPU awake. The interrupted parent trial is
explicitly marked invalid; its battery averages are not retained as improvements.

Battery recording now avoids GPU sensors even when active; `--gpu-telemetry` is
an explicit opt-in for thermal experiments. Drawer GPU reads are separately
paced beyond the autosuspend delay, with cached values and sample ages between
reads. The generic hwmon fan scan cannot bypass that pacing. New regression
tests cover already-active devices as well as sleeping ones.

## Thermal evidence boundary

The owner confirms the historical Diablo II shutdowns occurred on the original
ASUS barrel charger. That charger was reconnected for the current AC trials;
the external monitor remained disconnected. Its printed wattage was not read.

Initial Performance readback showed global/per-policy boost=1 and hardware
maximum 4,936,631 kHz, but scaling_max_freq stayed at 3,301,000 kHz. Direct writes
could lower the latter but could not raise it above 3,301,000. Toggling per-policy
boost and governors did not clear it. At idle, stopping the two policy services,
reinitializing amd-pstate through its supported passive/active interface and
restarting the services cleared the inherited constraint. Active mode remains
in use. Powersave and Performance then verified at 3,301,000 and 4,936,631 kHz
respectively. This does not establish the original constraint's cause. It is
not silently worked around with a driver reset on every mode transition.

A native TuneD sysfs instance now sets and verifies the driver's current legal
ceiling after selecting boost. The drawer also checks that readback, rather than
treating boost=1 as sufficient proof of boost availability.

The initial capped two-worker matrixprod run completed 300 s, peaked at 51 C,
and reported 1,912.85 bogo ops/s. With working boost and stock cooling, the same
load reached the conservative 82 C abort guard after 185.3 s; its 2,699.56 bogo
ops/s is about 41% faster, but is not a completed sustained stability result.
Both used Performance, the original barrel charger and the internal 120 Hz panel.
The dGPU slept throughout. Battery charging tapered during the capped run;
charging had stopped in the boosted run. Component power and CPU limits are
recorded separately from battery flow. Early AC summaries incorrectly projected
runtime from the charge-capped battery's tiny residual discharge: those values
are invalid. `summary-corrected.json` removes them and the recorder now rejects
runtime projections whenever any AC source is online.

The 4.00 GHz ceiling is the highest completed candidate here; this is not an
exhaustive search or proof of a mathematical optimum. It gives similar sustained
all-core throughput to 3.80 GHz under the APU budget, with more permitted light
workload frequency. The unrestricted setting's fast burst needs a separate
frequency ceiling: a successful PPT write/readback is not proof that short
bursts are constrained.

The final 100 samples of the 4.00 GHz and boost-off runs averaged 44.64 and
30.53 W respectively at the 680M hwmon sensor. The
[kernel's Rembrandt driver](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v7.2/drivers/gpu/drm/amd/pm/swsmu/smu13/yellow_carp_ppt.c)
maps this input to firmware `CurrentSocketPower`; it is not a pure CPU rail
measurement. Approximately 20% more synthetic throughput costs substantially
more reported socket power with the dGPU asleep. This informed boost-off
Balanced/Powersave and bounded-boost Performance. These readings are not battery
power or measured whole-laptop energy per task; do not add them to dGPU power
and call the sum total laptop consumption. A short
3.56-second software/QML check overlapped the boost-off run; precision beyond
these approximate tradeoffs would require dedicated repeated trials.

These CPU trials used the first earlier-response fan curve. Later GPU work
also increased the CPU fan's response to assist the shared cooling system.

## Implementation research

Current upstream source was inspected where accessible; revisions and downloaded
paths are preserved in local `2026-10-18/upstream/revisions.json`.

- [TuneD](https://github.com/redhat-performance/tuned) 2.28 installed source:
  native plug-in ordering, global sysfs verification/rollback, PPD holds and
  AC/DC mapping provide the required policy machinery. Retain this architecture
  and fix the observed boost interface mismatch rather than add another owner.
- [ASUS Linux](https://github.com/OpenGamingCollective/asusctl) main
  `15664e8e9177d54de57e9321f6baf972b9113712`: platform writes and fan-curve/PPT
  lifecycle are coupled. Current source includes fan-curve restoration fixes;
  installed 6.5.0 must be inspected separately before relying on custom curves.
- [Bazzite](https://github.com/ublue-os/bazzite) main
  `53d0facdc737381b6a1c6ec750d69909484203b1`: desktop compressed-swap policy
  and handheld hardware gating inspected. Its PowerStation helper excludes
  GA402; do not install handheld power owners. Existing bounded zstd zram remains.
- [CachyOS Settings](https://github.com/CachyOS/CachyOS-Settings) master
  `e27ea45cc5afa45d2454f7c06d57d30c30c5d569`: game-performance uses a standard
  PPD launch hold rather than permanent low-level tuning. Useful pattern;
  no scheduler or kernel transplant is justified by this machine's measurements.
- [Nobara session source](https://github.com/Nobara-Project/steamdeck-edition-packages)
  `98383d8ba707b8db26453e68e70f2ee3d3cd7fb1`: changes power saver to Balanced

- [ChimeraOS manifest](https://github.com/ChimeraOS/chimeraos/blob/master/manifest):

- [GameMode](https://github.com/FeralInteractive/gamemode/blob/master/example/gamemode.ini)
  `a74b8106a2236d1f2696aa44c93bc4c8ef13b42e`: governor/platform and optional
  GPU/sysctl actions overlap current owners. No new GameMode daemon installed.
- [TLP CPU implementation](https://github.com/linrunner/TLP/blob/main/func.d/10-tlp-func-cpu)
  `e5dce348c30d8a34be9b3fe1138ffd9f261ef933`: global boost and separate EPP/
